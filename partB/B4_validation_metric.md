# B4 — Validation Metric

I would monitor the serving stack's **sequence-preemption counter/rate**. For example, vLLM exposes a Prometheus preemption counter (`vllm:num_preemptions` in current versions; metric naming can vary by version/serving stack). Below the estimated ~25–26 concurrent 4096-token-sequence capacity, I would expect the preemption rate to remain **0 or near 0**. Once concurrency exceeds that threshold and KV-cache capacity becomes saturated, I would expect preemptions to rise sharply. This matches the benchmark: **0 preempted sequences at batch 24, 7 at batch 32, and 23 at batch 48**. A simultaneous throughput decline and rise in preemptions would support the KV-cache-saturation → preemption mechanism; if throughput degraded without increased preemptions, I would investigate other bottlenecks instead.
